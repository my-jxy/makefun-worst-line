# Brainstorming Candidate Pickup Lines

## Criteria Checklist & Confidence Score (1-5):
1.  **AI-like & Bad:** The line must sound like an AI trying (and failing) to be romantic.
2.  **Laugh/Groan:** It should be genuinely funny or clever-groan-worthy.
3.  **Enjoyable Badness:** The failure is the joke, not just confusing.
4.  **Machine-like Phrasing:** Flat, over-helpful, slightly ominous, not human.
5.  **One Line Joke:** Self-contained, no setup, memorable.

---

### Candidate 1: Over-literal compliance
**Line:** "Are you a 404 error? Because I can't find anything wrong with you, according to my last scan. Please provide feedback if this assessment is incorrect."
**Scores:**
*   AI-like & Bad: 4
*   Laugh/Groan: 4
*   Enjoyable Badness: 4
*   Machine-like Phrasing: 5
*   One Line Joke: 4
**Reasoning:** The compliance and the request for user feedback on a compliment makes it uniquely AI-bad and funny. The slight awkwardness of the phrasing adds to the machine-like quality.
